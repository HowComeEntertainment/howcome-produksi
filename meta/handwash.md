## SHORT (videos/handwash_short.mp4, 55 s)
Title: What If You Never Washed Your Hands After the Toilet? 🧼 #shorts

Description:
You leave the bathroom without washing your hands. 🧼 What happens?
In one study, only ~5% washed long enough to really kill germs, and ~1 in 10 didn't wash at all. 😳
Handwashing with soap can cut diarrhea illnesses by about a quarter to a third.
Plot twist: jet air dryers blew bacteria around much more than paper towels!

Be honest: do you always wash your hands? 👇

Sources: Borchgrevink et al., J Environ Health (2013) · CDC
