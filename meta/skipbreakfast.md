## SHORT (videos/skipbreakfast_short.mp4, 55 s)
Title: What If You Skipped Breakfast for 30 Days? 🍳 #shorts

Description:
"Breakfast is the most important meal of the day"... says who? 🤔
Week 1: your stomach growls on schedule. After a couple of weeks, that morning hunger fades.
Breakfast doesn't "kickstart" your metabolism, and a big BMJ review found breakfast eaters ate ~260 MORE calories a day. 😳
Plot twist: skipping only works if lunch isn't a junk food party.
