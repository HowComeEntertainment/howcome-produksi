## SHORT (videos/teeth_short.mp4, 55 s)
Title: What If You Never Brushed Your Teeth Again? 🦷 #shorts

Description:
No toothbrush. No toothpaste. Forever. What would happen? 😬
Within hours, bacteria build sticky plaque. In a few days it hardens into tartar that a toothbrush can't remove.
In a classic experiment, volunteers who stopped cleaning their teeth ALL got bleeding gums in 2-3 weeks.
Plot twist: ancient hunter-gatherers never brushed, yet had far fewer cavities. The difference? Our sugar. 🍬
