Title: What If You Never Cut Your Nails? 💅 #shorts

Description:
What if you NEVER cut your nails again? 💅
Fingernails grow ~3.5 mm a month. After a year, they'd be ~4 cm longer, and germs love hiding underneath.
Keep going for years and nails curl and twist. One woman's nails reached over 8 meters! 😳
Plot twist: nails don't grow after death. That's a myth!
