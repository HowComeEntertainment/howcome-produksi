## SHORT (videos/energydrink_short.mp4, 55 s)
Title: What If You Drank an Energy Drink Every Day? ⚡ #shorts

Description:
One can in the morning, one after lunch, one before the gym... what does that do to your body? ⚡
~160 mg caffeine + 50 g+ sugar in one big can. Caffeine only blocks your brain's "sleepy" signal, so when it wears off: the crash.
One study found a single energy drink raised blood pressure more than caffeine alone. 😳
Plot twist: drink one every day and you need it just to feel normal. Skip a day? Headache, fog, bad mood.
