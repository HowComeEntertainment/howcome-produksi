## SHORT (videos/sitallday_short.mp4, 55 s)
Title: What Happens If You Sit All Day, Every Day? 🪑 #shorts

Description:
Breakfast, car, desk, couch... sit, sit, sit, sit. 😅 What does that do to your body?
Your leg muscles are sugar sponges, and sitting puts them to sleep. After just 3 hours, blood flow in your leg arteries gets worse.
Plot twist: sitting is NOT the new smoking. A study of 1,000,000+ people found ~1 hour of brisk walking a day seemed to cancel out the extra risk of sitting 8 hours. 🚶
