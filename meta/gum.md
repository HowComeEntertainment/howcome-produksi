You swallowed your gum! 😱 Does it really stay in your stomach for 7 years?
Nope. The gum base can't be digested, but your gut is a conveyor belt. It usually leaves in a few days.
Real trouble is rare, mostly kids who swallowed lots of gum.
Plot twist: scientists got a full human DNA from 5,700-year-old chewed "gum"! 🧬
