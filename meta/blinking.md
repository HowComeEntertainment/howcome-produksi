## SHORT (videos/blinking_short.mp4, 55 s)
Title: What If You Stopped Blinking? 👁️ #shorts

Description:
Are you blinking right now? 👁️ Now you're thinking about it.
You blink 15-20 times a minute. Stop, and your eyes sting, burn, and dry out.
On screens, your blink rate can drop from ~18 to just a few per minute. That's why your eyes feel tired! 😵
Plot twist: right after a blink, your brain's resting network lights up. A tiny mental break!

Did you blink while watching? 👇

Sources: Patel et al., Optom Vis Sci (1991) · Nakano et al., PNAS (2013)
