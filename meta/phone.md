Title: What If You Checked Your Phone Every 5 Minutes? 📱 #shorts

Description:
Bzzz... you check your phone. Nothing. 5 minutes later, again. 📱

In one experiment, people did worse on brain tests just because their phone was on the desk, even turned off! Phone in another room? Better scores. 🧠
Plot twist: ever felt a buzz that wasn't there? That's phantom vibration.
