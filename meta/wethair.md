Title: What If You Slept With Wet Hair Every Night? 🛏️ #shorts

Description:
Wet hair, straight to bed. "You'll catch a cold!" 🛏️ True?
No: colds come from viruses, not wet hair.
But wet hair is weaker, so rubbing it on a pillow all night means more frizz and breakage. And damp pillows are a fungus hotel. 🍄
Plot twist: getting chilled might wake up a virus that's already there!
