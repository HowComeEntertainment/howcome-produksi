## SHORT (videos/water_short.mp4, 55 s)
Title: What If You Drank Only Water for 30 Days? 💧 #shorts

Description:
No soda. No juice. No sweet coffee. Only water for 30 days? 💧
One can of soda ≈ 39 g sugar (~10 teaspoons).
Liquid calories don't fill you up, so swapping sugary drinks for water can cut hundreds of calories a day without eating less. 😳
Plot twist: drinking huge amounts too fast can be dangerous. Sip, don't chug!

Could you do 30 days? Team water or team soda? 👇

Sources: CDC · Malik et al., Am J Clin Nutr (2006)
This video is for education only, not medical advice.
