## SHORT (videos/knuckles_short.mp4, 55 s)
Title: What If You Cracked Your Knuckles Every Day? 🤚 #shorts

Description:
Pop! Crack! You probably did it today without noticing. But does it really cause arthritis? 🤔
That pop is a gas bubble forming inside your joint (scientists filmed it in an MRI!). And it takes ~20 minutes before the same knuckle can pop again.
Plot twist: one doctor cracked ONLY his left hand for 60 years... no arthritis in either hand. He won an Ig Nobel Prize! 🏆
