One can in the morning, one after lunch, one before the gym. What if you drank an energy drink every single day?

A big can can pack around one hundred sixty milligrams of caffeine, and some have over fifty grams of sugar. That's more sugar than many adults should have in a whole day. And if you drink it in the afternoon? That caffeine can still steal sleep at night.

Here's the trick. Caffeine blocks adenosine, your brain's sleepy signal. But adenosine keeps piling up. When the caffeine wears off, it all hits at once. That's the crash.

In one study, healthy young adults drank a single energy drink, and their blood pressure jumped more than with caffeine alone. Some even showed changes in their heart rhythm.

Plot twist! Your body adapts. Drink one every day, and you start needing it just to feel normal. Skip a day? Headache, fog, bad mood. That's caffeine withdrawal.
