No toothbrush. No toothpaste. Forever. What if you never brushed your teeth again? Not even once?

Your mouth is home to hundreds of kinds of bacteria. Most are harmless, but some love sugar just as much as you do. And every time you eat, you feed them too.

Within hours, they build a sticky film called plaque. Leave it for a few days, and it hardens into tartar, so rough that a toothbrush can't remove it anymore. And your breath? Yikes.

In a classic experiment, healthy volunteers stopped cleaning their teeth. Within two to three weeks, every single one got swollen, bleeding gums. When they started brushing again, it healed.

Plot twist! Ancient hunter-gatherers never brushed, yet they had far fewer cavities than we do. The difference? They didn't eat our sugar and soft processed food.
