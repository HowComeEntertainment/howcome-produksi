Pop! Crack! You probably did it today without even noticing. What if you cracked your knuckles every single day? Would you really get arthritis?

Up to half of people crack their knuckles, often when they're bored or stressed. And for years, nobody even knew where that popping sound came from.

Your knuckle joints are filled with a slippery fluid. When you pull the joint apart, the pressure drops, and a gas bubble suddenly forms. Scientists filmed it inside an MRI. That's the pop!

And that's why you can't crack the same knuckle again right away. The gas needs about twenty minutes to dissolve back into the fluid. Your joint literally needs a recharge.

Plot twist! One doctor cracked the knuckles on only his left hand, twice a day, for sixty years. His result? No arthritis in either hand. He even won an Ig Nobel Prize.
