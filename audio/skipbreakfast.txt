Parents, teachers, cereal boxes... they all say breakfast is the most important meal of the day. But what if you skipped it for thirty days?

Fun fact: that famous line got a huge push from cereal companies over a hundred years ago. Yep. People selling breakfast said breakfast matters.

Week one is rough. Your hunger hormone, ghrelin, follows your schedule, so your stomach still growls at breakfast time. Cranky, foggy, dreaming of donuts. But after a couple of weeks, for many people, that morning hunger fades.

"Breakfast kickstarts your metabolism"? When researchers tested it, breakfast didn't change how many calories people burned at rest.

Here's the shock. A big BMJ review found breakfast eaters actually ate about two hundred sixty more calories a day, and breakfast didn't help people lose weight.

Plot twist! Skipping only works if lunch isn't a junk food party. And kids, teens, and people with diabetes on medication should be careful. What really matters is what you eat all day.
