# What Happens If You Skip Breakfast for 30 Days?

## SHORT (target 89 s)

They told you breakfast is the most important meal of the day. But what if you skipped it... for thirty whole days?

First, a little secret. That famous line got a huge push from cereal companies more than a hundred years ago. Yep. People selling breakfast told you breakfast was important.

So how come so many people skip it and feel totally fine?

Week one is the rough part. Your hunger hormone, ghrelin, follows your schedule. So at your usual breakfast time, your stomach still growls. You might feel cranky.

But for many people, the body adapts. After a couple of weeks, that morning hunger calms down, because your hormones learn the new routine.

What about weight? A big review in the BMJ found breakfast eaters actually ate about two hundred sixty more calories a day, and breakfast didn't help people lose weight.

Plot twist! Skipping only works if lunch doesn't turn into a giant junk food party. Many skippers overeat later and cancel it all out.

And it's not for everyone. Kids and teens usually focus better at school after breakfast. And people with diabetes on medication should talk to a doctor first.

So breakfast isn't magic, and skipping it isn't a crime. What really matters is what you eat all day.

## YOUTUBE (target 144 s)

They told you breakfast is the most important meal of the day. Your parents said it. Your teachers said it. Cereal boxes definitely said it. But what if you skipped it... for thirty whole days?

First, a little secret. That famous line got a huge push from cereal companies more than a hundred years ago. Yep. People selling breakfast told you breakfast was important.

So how come so many people skip it and feel totally fine?

Week one is the rough part. Your hunger hormone, ghrelin, follows your schedule. It rises right before the times you usually eat. So at your normal breakfast time, your stomach still growls. You might feel cranky, a little foggy, and very interested in donuts.

But for many people, the body adapts. After a couple of weeks, that morning hunger calms down, because your hormones learn the new routine.

Now, what about the classic warning, "Breakfast kickstarts your metabolism"? When researchers in England tested it, eating breakfast did not change how many calories people burned at rest. Breakfast eaters just moved around a bit more in the morning.

And weight? A big review in the BMJ found breakfast eaters actually ate about two hundred sixty more calories a day, and breakfast didn't help people lose weight.

But wait. Some studies link skipping breakfast to heart disease. How come? Those studies only watch people. They can't prove cause. And breakfast skippers, on average, also smoked more, drank more, and ate worse. So the breakfast may not be the real villain.

Plot twist! Skipping only works if lunch doesn't turn into a giant junk food party. Lots of skippers get so hungry they overeat later and cancel everything out.

And it's not for everyone. Kids and teens usually focus better at school after breakfast. And people with diabetes on medication should talk to a doctor first.

So breakfast isn't magic, and skipping it isn't a crime. What really matters is what you eat all day.

## Sources (for on-screen, small, bottom-left)
- Sievert et al., BMJ (2019): breakfast eaters ~260 kcal/day more, no weight-loss benefit
- Betts et al., Am J Clin Nutr (2014), Bath Breakfast Project: no change in resting metabolic rate, more morning activity
- Cummings et al., Am J Physiol (2004) / Frecka & Mattes (2008): ghrelin rises before habitual meal times
- Rong et al., J Am Coll Cardiol (2019): observational link, skippers had more smoking/drinking/poor diet
- Adolphus et al., Front Hum Neurosci (2013): breakfast and school performance in children/teens
- History: "most important meal" promoted by early-1900s cereal marketing (Lenna Cooper, Good Health, 1917)
